Privacy Policy

Theme Park Bites Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026

Last updated: April 17, 2026

Summary

  • You can use the app anonymously. No email or account is required to browse, save, or review.
  • Reviews you submit (score, optional text, optional photos, visit date) are shared so other fans can see them. That is the entire point of a review app.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your data at any time from the Profile screen.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Reviews. When you submit a review we store the score, optional written text, optional photos, and visit date. Reviews are visible to other users as community reviews.
  • Bookmarks. Menu items and venues you save are stored so you can access them across devices.
  • Reports. If you report a photo or flag a problem, we store that report so our moderators can review it.
  • Account (optional). If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple provides us a stable identifier and the email address you choose to share. You can use the app without signing in.

Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous user identifier. When you first open the app we create an anonymous user ID so your reviews and bookmarks persist. This ID is not linked to your real identity unless you choose to sign in with Apple.
  • Approximate location (only on request). If you tap "Use my location" we use your location to auto-select the nearest theme park. We do not store or share GPS coordinates.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. Screen views, taps, and feature use, aggregated and anonymized. Never includes photos, review text, or personal identifiers.
  • Crash diagnostics. Device model, OS version, and anonymized crash traces so we can fix bugs. Never includes personal content.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, health data, or photos beyond what you explicitly choose to upload to a review.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking. The app does not show ads.

How We Use the Information

  • To display your reviews and bookmarks inside the app and to other users (for reviews).
  • To keep the catalog fresh via community "I just ate this" confirmations and problem reports.
  • To moderate reported content and enforce community standards.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app using aggregate analytics and crash reports.

How the Information is Stored

We use Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, and Storage) as our backend. Review photos are uploaded to a Supabase Storage bucket in the United States. All database rows are protected by row-level security policies so users can only modify their own data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only with the service providers we use to operate the app (Supabase for storage and authentication; Apple for Sign in with Apple and crash reporting on iOS). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy standards.

Children

Theme Park Bites is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

  • Delete a review. Open the menu item, tap "Edit your review," then tap Delete.
  • Delete your account and data. In Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your reviews, bookmarks, reports, and anonymous ID from our backend.
  • Request a copy of your data. Email us using the support page below and we will provide it within 30 days.
  • Revoke location access. iOS Settings → Theme Park Bites → Location.
  • Disable analytics. iOS Settings → Privacy → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers.

Data Retention

We retain your reviews and bookmarks for as long as you keep an active account. Deleted reviews and deleted accounts are removed from our active database immediately and from backups within 30 days.

International Users

The app is operated from the United States. If you use the app from outside the United States, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States for processing.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Email support@themeparkshark.com or visit the support page.
